Shipping Policies

We're committed to delivering your orders quickly and safely.

Order Processing

Orders are processed within 1-2 business days after payment confirmation. You'll receive a confirmation email with your order details and tracking number once your order is shipped.

  • Orders placed on weekends are processed on the next business day.
  • During peak seasons, processing may take up to 3 business days.
  • You can track your order status from your account dashboard.

Packaging & Handling

All orders are carefully packed in high-quality, sustainable packaging to ensure your items arrive in perfect condition.

  • Eco-friendly materials used wherever possible.
  • Fragile items are packed with extra cushioning.
  • Gift wrapping options available at checkout.

Shipping & Delivery

We partner with leading courier services to deliver your orders across India. Delivery timelines vary based on your location and the shipping method selected.

  • Standard Shipping: 3-7 business days (Free above ₹499).
  • Express Shipping: 1-2 business days (₹199).
  • International Shipping: 7-14 business days (charges apply).

Safe & Secure

Your order is insured against loss or damage during transit. In case of any issues, our support team is ready to assist you.

  • All shipments are tracked end-to-end.
  • Signature required on delivery for high-value orders.
  • Contact us within 72 hours of delivery for any issues.
